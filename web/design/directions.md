# Six candidates, three selected for full visual drafts

All candidates use the same content and common spec. This is a visual exploration,
not six different product scopes. A user choice is pending. No design is approved.

## A. Evidence desk — selected, dir-1.html
Source: information-design principles associated with Edward Tufte; borrowed as
reasoning, without claiming an endorsement or copying a specific work.
Rules: large editorial left column, a believable quotation/evidence composition
on the right, warm-neutral white and dark ink, restrained forest-green action color.
System serif display plus system sans body. Wide margins, hairline rules, small
radius. Material: a quotation excerpt with the 229.00 price and its explanation.
Skeleton: asymmetric opening spread, then a calm linear story and an evidence table.
Bold choice: the document/evidence composition occupies nearly half the opening.
Form comes from keeping source and conclusion visibly beside one another.

## B. Quiet ledger — alternate thumbnail
Source: Naoto Fukasawa's principle of quiet, unobtrusive utility, interpreted for
this interface rather than a reproduction of industrial objects.
Rules: centered narrower reading column, soft gray divisions, compact serif headings,
paired sample/decision rows; all navigation remains obvious.
Material: a vertical list of the ten named sample packs.
Skeleton: centered promise above a compact review list.
Bold choice: large calm whitespace around a single active sample row.
Form comes from the repeated task of checking one quotation at a time.

## C. Review room — selected, dir-2.html
Source: https://linear.app, captured in references/board.jpg when available.
Rules: dark graphite, very fine borders, close-cropped functional UI, strong but
limited title scale; no decorative gradients, fake activity or status lights.
Material: an actual pack03 decision with the source number and a step list.
Skeleton: left narrative column and a wide horizontal review workspace below it;
results matrix and live-run controls have strong product-interface character.
Bold choice: the working review panel is the main opening visual.
Form comes from a fixed procedure that can be followed and inspected.

## D. Clear account — alternate thumbnail
Source: https://mercury.com, captured in references/board.jpg when available.
Rules: pale sage surface, dark readable serif heading, measured row spacing,
financial-document relationships and very restrained accent usage.
Material: sample result summary rather than a bank balance or invented transaction.
Skeleton: narrow title, broad light account-style results panel below.
Bold choice: muted large surface blocks containing the actual evaluation rows.
Form comes from careful financial review, without pretending this is a banking app.

## E. Open report — selected, dir-3.html
Source: Huashu style roulette, captured second 5: web entry 5 % 20 + 1 = 6,
Bold Big-Type Editorial; specimen web/06-bold-big-type.jpg.
Rules: oversized sans-serif headline, crisp blue/white flat surfaces, broad asymmetric
columns, strong alignment and an explicit 6/5/7/9 chart. Body text remains calm.
Material: the real four-approach comparison, as an editorial data graphic.
Skeleton: wide headline, full-width chart-led opening, a narrative sequence of
horizontal comparison rows, and a precise live-check workspace further down.
Bold choice: data typography is the opening visual instead of a quotation sheet.
Form comes from the experimental journey rather than the appearance of AI software.

## F. Layered register — alternate thumbnail
Source: same captured second 5: east entry 5 % 12 + 1 = 6, Hong Kong Street Signs;
specimen east/06-hk-street-signs.jpg. Adapted substantially to the restrained brief:
neon, saturated signs and ornamental density are omitted (under 70% stylistic fidelity).
Rules: compact aligned labels on overlapping horizontal document strips, high-contrast
ink, ivory, and one blue accent. No exotic glyphs or invented multilingual text.
Material: actual pack identifiers and full verdict labels.
Skeleton: layered horizontal review strips under a simple heading.
Bold choice: the review strips overlap in a controlled stack.
Form comes from a batch of supplier quotations arriving together.

Selection rationale: A, C and E have visibly different skeletons, distinct material
hierarchies and different visual temperatures. B and D are close to A; F's style
conflicts with the quiet mood and remains an alternate rather than being forced in.
